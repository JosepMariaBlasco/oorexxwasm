Worker threads lost when a worker terminates its workers and is terminated right after

Each round: the page starts a worker (parent.js); it creates a shared WebAssembly.Memory (64 MiB, maximum 512 MiB) and four workers of its own (child.js) that receive it. Then the parent terminates its four children and tells the page, and the page terminates the parent at once.

Needs cross-origin isolation (serve with serve.py).
